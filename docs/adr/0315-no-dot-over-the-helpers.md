# ADR 0315 · No dot over the helpers' heads

Date: 2026-11-15 · Status: accepted (owner, thread «Proporre nuova Grindhouse e Orchestrator», 2026-11-15: «Il pallino
non è perfettamente allineato … forse si può togliere dato che si capisce dall'animazione»; then «fallo togliere») ·
Amends: ADR 0313 (point 3)

## Decision

The coloured dot over each helper at a bench is gone. It was centred on the helper's box, which a tool in hand widens
on one side, so it sat off the body; and it repeated what the scene already says: at work the person works, waiting for
the user it shows «!», on an error its own scene, finished the check mark. The state in words stays on the helper's
name tag (hover or focus) and in the bench's foot counts.

## Consequences

- Who works at a bench is read from the people, the lit torch and the foot line («N in corso»).
